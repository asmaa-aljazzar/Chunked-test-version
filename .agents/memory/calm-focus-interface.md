---
name: Calm focus interface
description: Product design constraints for Chunked's focus and day-planning screens.
---

Chunked should help users focus without adding pressure or extra decisions. Use clear flows with sensible defaults; keep the current chunk easy to reach from either navigation layout. Use readable contrast in light and dark themes, and let hover/focus feedback draw from the active theme's accent color. Keep movement subtle and purposeful, offer reduced-motion support, and never autoplay ambient sound or visuals. Never move or delete unfinished tasks without the user's explicit choice.

For Focus-room backgrounds, use saved or uploaded image/video files only. Do not offer YouTube players or YouTube video backgrounds.

Focus-session start and end cues are separate from ambience and need their own user controls; background mute and volume should not control timer chimes.

**Why:** The product is meant to feel comfortable and uncluttered for people managing many interests without feeling exhausted or punished, and the user must remain in control of unfinished work.

**How to apply:** When changing the Day, Focus, or navigation UI, prefer one obvious next action, calm theme-matched feedback, and optional ambience that never blocks task or timer use. For the Focus-room background picker, use saved/uploaded media rather than YouTube embeds. Keep timer-event alerts independently controllable from ambient sound.
