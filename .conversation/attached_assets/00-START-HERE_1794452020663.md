# Chunked discovery handoff
Prepared for founder-led discovery with a senior engineering partner.

## What this pack is
This records an evolving idea from planning conversations. It is NOT a finalized PRD, approved architecture, final ERD, validated business model, or instruction to implement every feature.

The founder wants to start from zero with Replit and discuss the product before coding. They want thoughtful challenge, alternatives, and decisions made together. The existing prototype is evidence of earlier exploration, not a specification to reproduce.

## How to use it
1. Upload this entire folder or ZIP to Replit.
2. Also upload the current Miro ERD export if available. It is not bundled here because no editable export was supplied; screenshots in earlier conversations may be outdated.
3. Paste the entire contents of 01-OPENING-PROMPT.txt into the conversation.
4. Start with discovery. Do not approve implementation until the problem, first audience, central workflow, first milestone, and technical feasibility are clear.
5. Let Replit revise the documents after decisions, keeping changes and unresolved questions visible.

## Files
- 01-OPENING-PROMPT.txt: full instruction to the collaborating agent.
- 02-BUSINESS-AND-PROBLEM.md: hypotheses, audience, value, business direction, validation.
- 03-EXPERIENCE-AND-LOGIC.md: candidate workflows, domain rules, and edge cases.
- 04-AI-LAYER.md: companion concept, AI boundaries, tools, privacy, evaluation.
- 05-TECHNICAL-DIRECTION.md: desired stack, environment checks, architecture and delivery.
- 06-DISCOVERY-WORKBOOK.md: workshop sequence, decision log, roadmap and acceptance checklist.
- 07-DRAFT-ERD.dbml: editable 19-entity model reconstructed from discussions.
- 08-ERD-REVIEW-NOTES.md: relationships, optionality, omissions, unresolved modeling choices.

## Reading status
Use these labels:
- Founder preference: clearly expressed direction; discuss before changing.
- Working proposal: a suggested implementation, not a commitment.
- Open: unresolved or dependent on discovery.

Do not mistake the size of this pack for an agreed MVP. A feature being mentioned does not authorize building it.
