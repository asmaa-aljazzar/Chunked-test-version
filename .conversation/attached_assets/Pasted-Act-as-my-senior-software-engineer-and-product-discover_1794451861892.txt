ABOUT CHUNKED
The concept is a calm planning and personal-growth app. It may help people divide a day into manageable chunks, choose meaningful tasks, build habits and goals, and complete study/work sessions. A character-like AI companion may assist across the experience using text and eventually voice. We must discover which users need this, what problem it solves better than their current approach, and what the smallest useful release is.

FOUNDER PREFERENCES TO DISCUSS
- Laravel backend and React frontend; Inertia is the current preference.
- Arabic/English and RTL/LTR readiness.
- Day structures starting from prayer-based, time-of-day, work, school/university, or custom.
- User convenience, theme previews, sidebar/top navigation choice.
- DAY and current chunk are central.
- GROW includes goals and habits.
- FOCUS is a chosen direction with one active focus.
- Study/work sessions belong to chunks and are separate from FOCUS goals.
- Reflections and insights may work with AI.
- AI should feel like a helpful character, not merely a separate chat page.
- Plans and subscription checkout are intended; templates and community are excluded from the current direction.
- A React Native application is a future possibility, not current scope.
These are preferences, not proof that all belong in the first release.
