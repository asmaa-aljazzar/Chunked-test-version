# Business and problem brief — draft

## Starting concept
Chunked is envisioned as a calm daily-planning and personal-growth product. It combines a day divided into useful periods with tasks, longer-term goals, habits, focus, reflections, and optional AI assistance.

This is a direction to investigate, not a validated problem statement. Do not assume that combining more productivity features makes a stronger solution.

## Audience hypotheses — open
Possible early audiences:
- University students balancing classes, study, personal routines and obligations.
- People balancing work and personal development.
- People who prefer day planning around prayer times.
- People overwhelmed by rigid schedules or large task lists.

Choose a specific initial audience through discovery. These groups are not interchangeable. Prayer-based organization is one option, not a statement about every user's language, religion or preferences.

## Candidate problems — hypotheses
- A long list does not explain what is realistic to do now.
- Plans do not fit the person's actual daily rhythm.
- Large goals are difficult to translate into small next actions.
- Users lose context moving between planners, habit trackers and timers.
- Planning interfaces themselves feel demanding.
- Reflection data exists but does not help users make a useful adjustment.

Ask which problem occurs often, who experiences it, what they do today, and why their workaround is insufficient.

## Candidate value proposition
"Plan a manageable day around your rhythm, take the next small step, and learn what helps you follow through."

Potential differentiators to test:
- Configurable chunks reflecting actual routines.
- A focused current-chunk view instead of repeatedly scanning the whole day.
- Accessible interface preferences and language direction.
- An optional companion that helps with a concrete action in context.

Do not claim unique market positioning or demand without evidence.

## Candidate jobs to be done
- When my day feels crowded, help me choose a realistic next task.
- When a goal feels too big, help me make an actionable small step.
- When I start studying or working, help me stay with a manageable session.
- When plans repeatedly fail, help me understand the pattern without judgment.

## Business direction — preferences, not pricing decisions
A free/premium subscription model is under consideration. The founder wants plans and checkout retained in the product direction.
Open questions:
- What must be free for the core product to be useful?
- What paid value is sustainable and understandable?
- Which AI capabilities have ongoing cost?
- Does checkout need to work for the first academic milestone?
- Are demo payments, provider test mode, or live charges required?
- Will customers accept limits, and how are limits communicated?

No approved prices, quotas, revenue projections, provider, live payment launch or purchase authorization exist.

## Validation before feature expansion
Suggested quick checks:
1. Ask a few relevant users to describe the last time they struggled to plan a day.
2. Observe their current method rather than only asking if they like Chunked.
3. Show a low-detail DAY/current-chunk flow and ask them to plan one real day.
4. Test whether they understand chunks and how they recover when a task is unfinished.
5. Compare manual task breakdown with AI assistance for an actual task.

Record findings, counterexamples and changes. Small interviews guide decisions; they do not establish statistical market validation.

## Success measures — choose together
Candidate measures:
- A new user can create a day and complete one task without help.
- Users return to their plan on a later day.
- Users can explain the difference between DAY, GROW and FOCUS.
- Saved progress survives refresh and sign-in on another device.
- AI suggestions are useful, correctable and affordable.
- Assessment requirements are met with working demonstrations.

Agree observable goals and targets instead of inventing analytics or promising a usage rate.

## Scope boundaries
Excluded from current direction: template marketplace, template management, community.
Later possibilities: React Native mobile app, richer voice, optional 3D character.
Admin scope is open; user management or operational controls should serve actual needs.
